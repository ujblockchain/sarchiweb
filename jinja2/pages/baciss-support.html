About the Innovation Program

Why You Should Not Miss BACISS 2026

BACISS (Blockchain · AI · Cloud · Innovation · Startup School) is an immersive, hands-on innovation program designed to empower students, researchers, and entrepreneurs to build, innovate, and scale technology-driven ventures. Here is why BACISS is an unmissable experience:

Learning from Industry Partners

BACISS is packed with industry partners who will be teaching, offering support, and serving as panel judges. These include Binance, Avalanche Team 1, IEEE South Africa, Built to Scale, JPM Monarch, eFama, 22 on Sloane, and many more.

Lots of Cash Prizes to Win

BACISS 2026 comes with increased cash prizes of over R40k+ plus branded gift items from our ecosystem partners. You are not just learning and building, you also get the opportunity to win exciting prizes.

Mentorship During and After BACISS

We want you to learn and use that knowledge to build. We will be there all the way, from your first idea during the build sessions to deployment and feature additions, even after BACISS ends. We offer technical support to help bring your startup to life.

Everyone Can Learn

Whether you are a UJ student or not, in sport science, accounting, engineering, or in a non-science field, you can learn at BACISS. Whether you’re a beginner or a pro, an enthusiast or simply passionate about tech, you can learn. It is teachable and it is learnable.

100% Free, Truly Free

It is free to attend and free to learn. All you simply need is a laptop, a notebook, and a pen to learn. No hidden registration fees or paywalled resources.

A Team Ready to Offer Support

Throughout BACISS and beyond, the UJ Blockchain team will be on hand to offer technical support to bring your idea to life. We have facilitators on the ground to help you, and even after BACISS you can still reach our team via email for technical support.
